Our story

We want every student to know what they're signing up for

Every year, high school students are asked a very large question: what do you want to do with your life? Most have never spent a day in the careers they are choosing between. Some feel lost because nothing has clicked yet. Others are certain of their path but have never tested it.

WhatIF is a simulation platform that lets students experience a realistic day in a career: the decisions, the pressure, the routines and the rewards. We believe a few minutes of hands-on experience can teach more than a career day pamphlet, and that every student, not only those with connections, deserves that chance.

What we believe

01

Exploration over pressure

There is no "right" career result. The goal is to help students learn what engages them, not to rank them.

02

Honest, not glamorous

Every simulation includes a reality check on the less visible parts of the job, so students see the whole picture.

03

Access for everyone

By working through schools, we can reach students who would never have the chance to shadow a professional.

Who it's for

Students who don't yet know what they want. Try several careers in an afternoon and notice which days you enjoyed most.

Students who already have an idea. Test your assumptions and discover what the job involves beyond the highlight reel.

Counselors and teachers. Gain a tool that prompts real conversations about interests, strengths and next steps.

For schools

WhatIF is offered to schools as an annual subscription covering all of a school's students. We are holding pilot conversations with schools now and welcome feedback on what would make the platform most useful for your counselors and classrooms.

  • Schoolwide student access in the browser, with no installation
  • New career simulations added regularly
  • Student reflection journals that support advisory and college and career counseling

Pricing and pilot terms to be finalized.
