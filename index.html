Most students choose a path from the few jobs they've seen up close

A job title says little about the actual day: the decisions, the pressure, the routines, the trade-offs. Students who are undecided need a way to explore. Students who think they know need a way to test that idea.

How it works

Each simulation runs about 15 to 20 minutes and follows a few days of working life, with real people, a paycheck and bills.

01

Choose a career

Select from business, sports, health, technology, creative fields and more. No prior knowledge is needed.

02

Make the decisions

Talk with coworkers, coaches and clients. Earn a salary, pay your bills and decide what to spend. Be late or make a mistake and it costs you.

03

Reflect and compare

Review how the day went, read an honest look at the profession, rate how much you enjoyed it, and build a journal of what engages you.
